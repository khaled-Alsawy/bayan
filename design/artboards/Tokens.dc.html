WHAT CHANGED

  • ink ramp repaired. ink-soft and ink-faint were within 3% of each other, so the three-step ramp was really two. Their measured contrast on paper is 6.91:1 and 4.64:1 respectively.
  • shadow-card dropped. A 42px shadow on every evidence card is what made five verses read as five search hits. Containment is now a 1px border; the one shadow left is on the floating "back to the answer" pill.
  • danger softened, focus darkened. #9f3f36 → #8f382f and #2e8b74 → #1b6f5c; focus measures 5.95:1 on paper. Nothing in the UI uses danger: a partial answer is not an error.
  • two type tokens added. --script (Amiri) and --sans (IBM Plex Sans Arabic). Naskh means retrieved; sans means generated. That is the policy's "different type treatment", enforced by a token rather than by discipline.
  • dark is a hue rotation, not an inversion. The dark grounds keep the paper's warm hue, so brass and emerald stay recognisable; emerald and emerald-deep swap roles, since #12614f is unreadable on a dark ground.
